## What

The Tauren druid chains for Cure Poison (Lessons Anew 6126 to 6130, level 14) and Aquatic Form (26, 28, 30 and 31, level 16) are limited to Tauren in the pack.
The pack has nothing of that kind for a Windshaper, and docs/CLASS-QUESTS.md lists only the Windshaper's Bear Form and Cat Form chains. The Skyborne guide
says nothing about where a Windshaper learns the two forms: its first step ("Read this first") says to ask the druid trainer.

## Why

Class quests are mandatory content (CLAUDE.md); if a Windshaper has quests for these, the guide is missing them, and if it learns the spells at the
trainer, the guide should say so.

## Done when

A Windshaper druid at levels 14 and 16 (play, or a recording from the friend who plays a druid) shows whether there are quests or only a trainer; the
guide and `Classes/DRUID.lua` get the steps, or the "Read this first" note is replaced by the fact.
