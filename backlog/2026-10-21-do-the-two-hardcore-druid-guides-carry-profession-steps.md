## What

The profession tracks have steps for the Troll route only: each step with a position is gated `.race Troll`. The Tauren Druid and Skyborne Druid guides
have no `#professions` header, so a character of theirs gets no profession steps. The user's Release 1 minimum names "all professions" for the Troll
Hunter only.

Should the two HARDCORE druid guides carry profession steps?

## Why

If yes, their trainers are in the pack (Thunder Bluff, Mulgore, Zephras Isle's own NPCs) and the tracks gain steps gated `.race Tauren` and
`.race Skyborne`, plus a `#professions` header in each guide. If no, nothing to do. It changes the scope of the druid chapters (more steps, more places
a hardcore character walks).

## Done when

- The user has answered, and the answer is in the guides (a `#professions` header and gated track steps) or here (no, with the reason).

## Notes

- Recommendation: no profession steps in the HARDCORE routes for Release 1. A hardcore character has no use for the detours, and the friend who runs
  one is collecting data, not following a profession plan. Revisit after launch.
