## What

The regenerated Troll Hunter guide routes Earthen Echo 98823 and, a chapter later, four Gloomrise quests (the Gloomrise nest in northern Mulgore is new in Forever), and works The Broodmother (96261, level 31,
a named boss with no rank in the data) at level 28. `docs/CLASS-QUESTS.md` suspects 98823 is the druid weapon quest, and the Tauren guide omits it; if a hunter is not offered it, two steps stall. PLAUSIBLE, not
proof. Found by the independent review R-guides-A (batch 1).

## Done when

- A hunter in the game is (or is not) offered 98823 and the Gloomrise quests (the audit tool `tools/audit_quests.py` on recorded play answers it), the guide is regenerated with the right set, and the named boss is
  judged for a normal route.
