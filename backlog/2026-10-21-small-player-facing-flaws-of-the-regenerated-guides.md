## What

Found by the independent review R-guides-A (batch 1) in the regenerated guides:

- 27 Troll and 19 Tauren `.todo` lines read "objective N has no position in the pack": developer text that tells the player nothing (the pack cannot fill them; the line could say "find it in the quest log").
- The hunter pet step (the first Troll chapter) has 11 lines, one over the step card's ten-line cut-off.
- troll-hunter-06 step 1 pins a hand-in 132 yards from its ender (known: the pack's position of the ender).
- The Troll route hops Mulgore, Stonetalon, Mulgore.

## Done when

- The `.todo` wording is in plain words for a player, the 11-line step fits or is split, and the others are either fixed or written off with a reason.
