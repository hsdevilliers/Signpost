## What

Found by the independent review R-guides-B (batch 1). The island's north camp is visited twice (steps 53 to 55 and 66 to 73 of the first chapter); Ashenvale gets a single-quest chapter after the Undercity ride and a second visit later;
the Barrens are visited four times, Thunder Bluff and Stonetalon three times each; 15 walk placeholders say "find your own way", mostly on Barrens roads (zones for levels 10 to 20).
