## What

Found by the independent review R-guides-B (batch 1), the first from Classic memory. The route sends a level 22 HARDCORE character into Ambermill Keep (the Dalaran casters of Silverpine): the Dalaran Digest book
(about 63.5,63.2, inside the keep) and 479 carry no note, only The Weaver (480) does. Forever has no rank for its own creatures in the pack, so the mainland ones (Rath'mael, Bruuz, Chol'aruk) are skipped by hand-kept rules, but the
island routes Commander Belguilos (92645) and Belathaan Brightwish (93746), both on the exit path, Jorel Windsinger and Naaleos (94489), and the Strange Hermit (93159) with no note; Echeyakee and Ishamuhale (rare, rank 4) have none
either. Every ranked elite item source does carry a note.
