## What

The regenerated Tauren HARDCORE guide spends 5h20 of its 27h06 on level-gate grinding: four `.level` gates, at 18 (72 minutes, 13,000 experience), 21 (74 minutes), 29 (151 minutes, 38,000 experience,
about 210 kills) and 30 (22 minutes). Each gate is HARDCORE's +1 margin (a quest is accepted or worked on at most one level above the character), which no step says. Examples: the level 18 gate sits before
quest 6543, which needs level 17 but is itself level 19; the level 29 gate unlocks The Lost Pages (level 30), whose three objectives have no position in the data. The level 21 grind sits in Moonglade
after the 25-minute trip, though its own quests need only level 20 (it is there for Undercity quests two chapters on); PLAUSIBLE (Classic memory): Moonglade has no level-20 creatures, so the player has to
leave to grind. The user accepted grinding at a gate on condition that the step says how much (the grind note does).

## Why

A player who reads "grind 38,000 experience" without a reason will doubt the guide. The reason is one line.

## Done when

- A gate's note names the quest that needs the level and says it is the HARDCORE margin; the planner does not place a grind in a zone without suitable creatures (or says where to go); the two
  quests with no objective position are not what a 151-minute grind is bought for.
