## What

The Skyborne HARDCORE route (generated by the Route Planner, `Guides/HordeWindshaperDruid.lua` + `Guides/Chapters/windshaper-druid-*.lua`) leaves Zephras Isle at about level 12 with a
placeholder step ("no route"): the way off the island is not in the route. The only link the data pack has is the island story chain 93836, 93090, 95349 ("The Earthen Ring"), which
ends in Mulgore. The planner prices the exit as a flat 15 minutes and skipped about 30 island quests. With 95349 made mandatory (A1's experiment) the beam total goes from 28h52 to
41h13 and the character stays on the island until about level 15. Nobody has checked in the game how a Skyborne character really leaves the island, or at what level.

## Why

A route that cannot be followed from its first chapter to its second is not valid (CLAUDE.md: a route is valid only if the game would offer every quest where the guide accepts it; and travel is
explicit). The two ways to fix it differ a lot in cost: a planner option that makes named quests mandatory ("required quests", usable for any guide, also for class quests) changes the
whole Skyborne route and adds hours; a hand-written island chapter keeps the planner's route and states the exit by hand. Which one is right depends on the real rule of the game.

- The route is changed to match: the planner takes the exit quest(s) as mandatory (new option, with a planner test) or the island chapter is written by hand with real steps, and the
