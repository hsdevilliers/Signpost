The pack now flags escorts and events (scripted). A normal route prices them and writes a note; a HARDCORE route does the same with a risk note, and only the hand-kept list in tools/hardcore_rules.json skips a quest (863 and 898 for now). HARDCORE.md's policy is to skip escorts, so the question is whether a HARDCORE route should leave out every scripted quest that is not a class quest or a prerequisite, or keep pricing and noting them. On a copy of the pack with the flag, a greedy plan routes 5 to 7 of them in each HARDCORE guide: Escorting Erland, Vorsha the Lasher, Torek's Assault, Gerenzo's Orders and Pyrewood Ambush, and for the Tauren the three Cleansing quests in Mulgore (Winterhoof, Thunderhorn, Wildmane); the planner already leaves out the slow ones (The Escape, Protect Kaya, Freedom to Ruul). Recommendation: skip them in HARDCORE (an escort NPC can be ambushed and an event can summon a fight; the data cannot say what comes) and keep them in a normal route. Done when the user has decided and the planner follows it.
