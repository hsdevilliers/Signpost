## What

Meal Appeal, Favorite Food, Rocks to Rests, Yelmak's Medley (an Expert Alchemist) and Whuut's the Rush are Forever quests given by profession NPCs in Orgrimmar. Unlike the gated Camping 101 family they carry no
skill field in the pack, and both the Troll Hunter and the Tauren guide route them. If the game gates them behind a profession or a skill, four to seven steps stall. The three Camping 101 profession quests
(First Aid, Leatherworking, Skinning: 97903, 97906, 97908) stay unrouted (known). Found by the independent review R-guides-A (batch 1).

## Done when

- At the first Orgrimmar visit (or from recorded play) it is seen whether the game offers the five to a character without the profession; the guides are fixed if not.
