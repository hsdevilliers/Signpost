## What

Found by the independent review R-guides-B (batch 1). The notes on the Moonglade quest 94913 and on 98340 in the Skyborne guide call it "a long walk" and name "a flight master at Nighthaven" as a way there. From Classic
memory a flight path cannot be used before it has been discovered, and no step teaches the Nighthaven node for the return. The legs themselves are worded safely (Teleport: Moonglade if you have it, VERIFY(beta), else
not in the data; do not walk it).
